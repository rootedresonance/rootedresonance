Why breath

Breathing is the only part of the autonomic nervous system you can operate deliberately. That makes it the most direct handle most people have on their own state, and the reason a practice you can do in ninety seconds can change an afternoon.

This is not about breathing correctly. It is about knowing which pattern does what, so you have options when you are activated instead of only afterward.

What we cover

  • Reading your own state: activation, shutdown, and the window between them.
  • Down-regulating patterns for anxiety, and up-regulating ones for numbness and freeze.
  • What is happening physiologically, in language that is accurate rather than mystical.
  • Building a practice small enough that you will actually keep it.
  • When to stop, which matters more than most instructors admit.

Safety, honestly

Strong breathwork is not neutral. Some patterns are contraindicated in pregnancy, cardiovascular conditions, seizure disorders, glaucoma, and certain psychiatric presentations, and intense practices can surface material you did not plan on. Tell me your history and we will build something appropriate. Slowing down is a legitimate outcome.

Scope and logistics

Sessions are ninety minutes at $75, available anywhere. Breathwork is a wellness and educational service, not psychotherapy, diagnosis or crisis care, and no therapeutic relationship is created by booking it.
