What it is like

You do not need to know what you are doing.

01

Arriving

A short conversation. Where you are today, what your body is holding. No history intake required.

02

Settling

You lie down, fully clothed, supported. Breath slows before anything else begins.

03

Sound

Bowls, voice and tone move through the room and through you. Sensation shows up where you had stopped noticing it.

04

Returning

Slow re-entry, water, and space to say anything that surfaced, or to say nothing and let it settle.

What happens in a sound session

You lie down. I play singing bowls, chimes and voice, and your nervous system does the rest. There is nothing to perform, nothing to report back, and no requirement that you feel anything in particular. Some people drop into deep rest. Some cry. Some fall asleep, which is not a failure.

Sustained tone gives the nervous system something steady to organize around. For people who have spent years explaining their inner life, an hour where no explanation is requested is often the point.

Who reaches for this

  • People who are exhausted by talking and want something that is not another conversation.
  • Anyone whose sleep, focus, or baseline calm has gone sideways.
  • People in therapy elsewhere who want a regulation practice alongside it.
  • Anyone curious, with no particular problem to solve.

What to expect and what to bring

Sessions run about sixty minutes at $60. Wear something you can lie still in. You do not need experience, a meditation practice, or any belief about how sound works. Afterward, most people want water and a slow twenty minutes before returning to the day, so plan accordingly.
