What somatic therapy actually is

Somatic therapy treats the body as a source of information rather than a problem to manage. Talk therapy works on the story you tell about your life. Somatic work goes underneath it, to the physical patterns that formed before you had words, and that keep running whether or not you understand them.

In practice that means slowing down. Noticing where a sentence lands in your chest. Staying with a sensation long enough for it to change on its own instead of being explained away. It is less dramatic than people expect and more effective than most of them are prepared for.

Who this tends to help

  • People who have been in therapy before and left with excellent insight and unchanged behavior.
  • People carrying developmental or relational trauma that predates language.
  • Chronic anxiety, freeze, shutdown, or a nervous system stuck on high alert.
  • Anyone who intellectualizes as a first defence, and knows they are doing it.
  • LGBTQIA+, neurodivergent, kinky, and ethically non monogamous clients who want a therapist who does not need the basics explained.

What a session looks like

We talk, but not the whole time. I will ask what you notice in your body, and if the answer is nothing, that is useful information rather than a wrong answer. We work at the pace your nervous system can tolerate, which is a real limit and not a matter of willingness. Some sessions are conversational. Some are quiet.

Licensing and logistics

Somatic therapy is psychotherapy, provided under my Licensed Professional Counselor credentials. I can work with residents of Ohio, Arizona and Colorado. Sessions are $150. I am in network through Headway and Rula for several plans, and the full list is on the FAQ page. You do not need a diagnosis or a referral to start.
