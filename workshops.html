Group workshops and authentic community

Why a group, when individual work exists

You can become very good at knowing yourself alone. The trouble is that adaptation is a social reflex, so it only shows up when other people are in the room. A group is the only setting where you can watch yourself start to perform and choose not to, in real time, with witnesses.

That is uncomfortable, and it is also the fastest route to self trust that holds under pressure.

What a workshop involves

  • Practicing directness without rehearsing it first.
  • Noticing the pull to manage how you are landing, and letting it pass.
  • Somatic and breath practices done together rather than alone.
  • Being received without having to earn it.
  • Agreements about confidentiality and consent, set before anything else.

Who these are for

Queer, neurodivergent, kinky and non monogamous folks, people in relationships of every configuration and out of them, and anyone tired of being the most self aware person in a room where nothing changes. No prior group experience is needed.
